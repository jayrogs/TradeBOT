Trend rides, picked at random

The rule: the chart is in an uptrend and a new higher low forms. Buy the next bar. Skip it if price already ran too far above that low. When you're up double what you risked, sell a third. When the uptrend ends, sell the rest. The uptrend ends when price wicks under the last higher low, or a lower high or lower low forms.

Five random trades for each timeframe, 5 minute up to weekly. Only names that trade at least $20M a day. Not chosen for how they turned out. The green and red background is what the rule could see at the time, so a buy always sits in green. Write under any chart you'd never take, or would have held longer. I read those.
