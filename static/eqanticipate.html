The EQ, anticipated

My EQ detector waits for two higher lows and two lower highs, and by then about three bars are left. Dan does not wait. From his videos, in my words: after a big move, if the first swing back takes half of it or more, an EQ is the likely pattern — so he scouts the higher low right then, on a much smaller chart, and sells near the lower high. If the swing back is small (38% or less) it is a flag, and buying the floor gets stopped out. This page is MY VERSION of that. It is here to be graded, not believed.
1. The 4-hour chart: A where the move began, B its low, C the swing back. C stays under A.
2. The move A to B is 4 or more normal bars. C takes back 50% or more of it.
3. Three ways in. One buy: inside the 15-minute candle at the FIRST touch of RSI 30 after C, stop under B. Two buys: the same, plus a second buy waiting at RSI 20, only at a better price. Confirm: wait for the higher low to confirm on the 4-hour, buy the next open, stop a wick under it, only when the sell line is at least 1x the risk away.
4. Sell it all just under C. (They sell a little before the level, never at it.)
