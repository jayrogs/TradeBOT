EQ

"eq is a series of HL and LH increasingly tightening"

Higher lows pressing up into lower highs pressing down, the gap closing, until price has to leave. The old page looked for a flat-topped, flat-bottomed box: that is a channel, kept separately.

the rule, as coded

the runevery low higher than the last, every high lower than the last. An equal one does not break the run.
how manyat least two higher lows and two lower highs.
tighteningthe last pair must be closer together than the first.
the edgesthe floor is the last higher low, the ceiling the last lower high. Each holds flat until the next one replaces it.
the breaka wick through a line, deeper than an equal low would be. Your rule, not a close.
when you can see itnothing counts until the last pivot confirms, two bars after it prints.

how many there are

Across the 40 focus names, four years. The shape takes about the same 25 bars to build on every chart size, which is a good sign it is not tuned to one. What changes is how often it happens.
