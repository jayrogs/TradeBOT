The BackBurner, the Chart Guys' way

You said I kept jumping to conclusions about this, and you were right: for a week I tested "RSI under 30" on any name at any time. This page is the trade as Dan teaches it himself — read from his own videos and the TCG e-books — and what happens to the numbers when each of his rules is switched on.
1. The name just had a big run to a new high. You missed it. You do not chase it — you put it on the back burner.
2. You buy the FIRST time RSI touches 30 after that high — inside the candle, at the touch. A second buy waits at 20.
3. The bigger uptrend is still intact. The drop is not news or earnings. The name trades heavily. Regular hours only.
4. No stop until the half is sold (Dan as written, and your round-1 note: "it feels really bad to sell while it's oversold"). Only a wide disaster line until then. When the bounce reaches the 12 EMA, sell half and put the stop under the low of the drop.
5. The rest is for the old high. The dip on the hourly is there to mark the daily higher low.
Round 3: same 16 charts, the stop Dan describes. Round 1 put a stop 3 normal bars under the fill on the buy bar, and a waterfall candle went straight through it (JBHT, MNST). Round 2 armed a stop as soon as RSI crossed back over 30, which was too early.
