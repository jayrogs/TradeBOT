The first EQ study bought every floor no matter what the bigger chart was doing, and bought a later touch of the floor rather than the higher low itself. This is your version. The shape is the same EQ as on /eqcoils.

the trade, as coded

which wayfrom the bigger chart. Every EQ is tested both ways and each trade is marked as taken with the bigger chart, against it, or with no trend there.
the buya higher low confirms inside the EQ: buy the next open. Short side: a lower high confirms, short the next open.
the stopa wick through that higher low (or over that lower high), deeper than an equal low would be.
the free rideat the lower high, sell the share that makes the rest unable to lose.
the restif it breaks through our way, trail 5 normal bars behind the best close.
careful readinga bar that touches both the stop and the lower high counts as stopped. Costs are out.

what counts as the bigger chart

Several readings, because "the higher timeframe shape" can be read more than one way. I checked each against eight trends nobody would argue about (NVDA down in May 2022 and up in 2023 and 2024, TSLA down late 2022, BTC down mid 2022 and up late 2023, META down 2022, AAPL up 2023). The daily against its 50 EMA got all eight right, and so did the daily against its 200 EMA. Every reading built from swing highs and lows missed several: inside a big decline the small pivots flip on every bounce. The page opens on the daily 50 EMA; click another to compare.

how you get in

After the higher low confirms buys the next open two bars after the low printed, which is when you can first know it was a higher low. By then price has often bounced most of the way to the lower high. The three limit entries rest a buy order down in the EQ instead: at the floor, a quarter of the way up the box, or halfway up. They fill only if price comes down to them, the stop is a wick under the floor, and the target is the lower high. Shorts are the mirror.

the answer

Per trade after costs. The big number is the average, because that is the money you actually make over many trades. Under it is the middle trade, what an ordinary trade looks like. free ride is how often price reached the lower high before the stop. The two can disagree in two ways and both matter: ! the average is positive only because a few big winners carry ordinary trades that lost; ~ the ordinary trade wins, but the losses are bigger than the wins, so the average does not.
