what your notes changed

the partial"wtf 91% sold? who is deciding this?" The old rule sold whatever share made the rest risk free. When the lower high sat right on top of the entry, that meant selling almost everything. Now it sells a fixed third or half, and the rest's stop moves to the entry. The old rule stays in the table for comparison.
pivots on top of each other"a HL then an LH in the next candle ... thats an anomaly, not an eq" Now the pivots must be at least 3 bars apart. The old rule (any spacing) stays in the table.
after-hours candles"was this after hours?" Yes: V 4h had 13 of 17 bars outside regular hours, ORCL 1h 18 of 31, NVDA 5m all 23 of 23 at 6% of normal volume. Stocks and ETFs are now also run on regular-hours bars only.
a buy after a higher high"did this buy, after a HH?" A bug. On INJ, price spiked through the ceiling in the two bars before the EQ could be known, and the EQ was still declared. An EQ that already broke is no longer an EQ.
where it is on the daily"i cant really tell where this is on the right side daily chart" The EQ is now an orange box on the daily panel, labeled, and the daily shows fewer bars so they are wider.

the trade, as coded

which waythe daily chart over its rising 50 EMA = long, under its falling 50 EMA = short (weekly for a daily EQ). Every EQ is still tested both ways, to show what "against" costs.
the buya higher low confirms inside the EQ: buy the next open. Short: a lower high confirms, short the next open.
the stopa wick through that higher low (or over that lower high), deeper than an equal low would be.
the far linethe lower high for a long, the higher low for a short. Part is sold there.
the restin the drawings (round 3, from your notes "why did it never sell" and "gave a ton back"): the stop walks up under each new higher low, and the rest is sold into overbought or on a close through the 12 EMA. The tables above still show the older exits for comparison. 5 and 15 minute stock trades close at the bell, and there is no buy on the last bar before it.
careful readinga bar that touches both the stop and the far line counts as stopped. Costs are taken out.
