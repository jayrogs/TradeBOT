You don't have to paint every bar — just the stretches you're confident about.

The background is the trend read, your rules: a green block opens when a HL and HH have both formed and runs until the first LH or LL; red is the mirror; EH / EL neither build nor kill; the killing pivot seeds the next pattern. The labels show every pivot so any block can be checked against the pivots that made it.

Blue shading = a range (HL LH HL LH, equal highs and lows count), living beside the trends — each one stretches back over the four pivots that birthed it, so its evidence is inside the blue. Blue over red reads purple: a range inside a downtrend. The thin "engine" lane at the bottom is the old per-bar machine, kept only for reference.
