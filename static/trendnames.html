Every name run through the same trend read the charts use, over the last 4 years. Up and down are how much of the time the chart was in an uptrend or a downtrend. Uptrend length is the average days one lasts, uptrend gain is what it made from open to death. Clean is how much of an uptrend closed above the 12 EMA. Ride is what your rule made per trade on this name: second higher low, trail once up 3x the risk, no partial. Grade: A rides better than the benchmark in both halves of the 4 years, B in one half, C about the same, D worse, F loses money. The benchmark is BTC for crypto, SPY for stocks and ETFs, ES for futures (highlighted). Moves is how far the name travels per 1% of the benchmark. Click a name for every trade it had, with charts.
