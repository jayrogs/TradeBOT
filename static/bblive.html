The same trade code the tests run, on live data, every 15 minutes. Stocks from Polygon (about 15 minutes late), crypto from Coinbase / Kraken, commodity futures from Databento (up to about 7 hours ago) plus Yahoo for the last hours. Read-only: nothing here places an order.

Armed — every condition is met, the RSI-30 touch has not happened yet

The buy rests at the price where the hourly RSI would touch 30 in the next hour. That price moves every hour, so it is only good until the next refresh. The later buys go on top from spare cash if price keeps falling.

Open — bought, not finished

Half sells when the bounce reaches the hourly 12 EMA. After that the stop sits under the low of the drop and walks up under each higher low; the rest sells on an hourly close under the last one. While the hourly RSI is 30 or under only the wide safety line can end a trade.
The rules this runs: first hourly RSI-30 touch after a 10%+ daily run to fresh highs, weekly trend intact · stocks skip company news / earnings days · stocks and commodity futures buy the whole bucket at 30, another bucket at 20 on top · crypto buys the bucket at 30, 2x at 25, 3x at 20, at most 3 buckets in one coin · half at the hourly 12 EMA, the rest walked under higher lows · crypto sells a quarter more 3 normal bars above the buy · 5 trades at once.
