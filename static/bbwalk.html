The oversold bounce, checked properly

RSI 14 on the 5m or 15m drops to 30 or under, then crosses back over. Buy the next open. Stop at the nearest structure that kills the idea. Half off at 1x the risk. The rest with the stop walked under each new higher low on the idea chart (the hourly for a 5m entry, the 4-hour for a 15m). Only taken when price is on the right side of that chart's 12 EMA. Shorts mirror it.

This setup first printed +1.32% a trade. That number was two bugs of mine: a partial booked on trades that never reached it, and a stop measured against a level the trail had already moved past.
