Trend study: which names trend cleanly

What this page answers: which names make clean, long trends, and which ones start a trend and quit.

How it counts. A trend starts when a name makes a higher low and then a higher high (or the mirror for a downtrend). From there we count every extra step it takes: each new higher low or higher high is one step. The trend ends when a wick breaks the last higher low, or a lower high and lower low show up. A trend that ended with zero extra steps is a fakeout: it started and quit.

How to read a row. "3.1 steps, 20% fakeouts, ran 6 bar-sizes" means: once this name starts a trend, it usually takes about 3 more steps, one in five trends quits right away, and the average trend travelled about 6 normal bars from start to top. Score is steps times distance for each trend (counting the two steps that started it), averaged over all its trends, and it is the default order: the top of the list is the names whose trends keep stepping and go far. A bar-size is the average height of one bar on that chart (the last 14 bars, high to low), so 6 bar-sizes on a daily chart is about six normal daily bars stacked up.

The right side is your trend ride on this name (buy the second higher low, sell when the last higher low breaks): trades, per trade, and a grade against BTC, SPY or ES.
