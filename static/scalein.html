Scaling into the dip, checked properly

The backburner, your way: buy it at or under 30, and keep buying as it drops. RSI 14 on the 5m or 15m reaches 30 or under — a first unit goes on at the next open, and up to two more as price keeps falling, each a further half a normal bar down, for as long as the print stays at or under 30. The position is the average of those fills. Stop at the nearest structure below the lowest fill. Half off at 1x the risk. The rest with the stop walked under each new higher low on the idea chart (the hourly for a 5m entry, the 4-hour for a 15m). Shorts mirror it.

Strength is measured against the name’s own sector leader, not against SPY for everything: each name is matched to the leader it actually moves with (NVDA to XLK, XOM to XLE, every coin to BTC, and so on), using only the first half of its history so the map cannot peek at what gets traded. 709 of 781 names get a leader; the inverse ETFs and the farm futures do not move with any sector and are left out rather than compared to noise.

Two earlier numbers on this page were wrong and are gone: an +1.32% a trade that was two bugs of mine (a partial booked on trades that never reached it, and a stop measured against a level the trail had already moved past), and a bounce trigger I invented — waiting for RSI to cross back over 30, which is not the rule.
